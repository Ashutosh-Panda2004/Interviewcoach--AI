# Code Of Conduct

InterviewCoach welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and focus criticism on the work.
- Make space for questions, differing perspectives, and good-faith mistakes.
- Protect user privacy and never share credentials or personal interview data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publication of private data is not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
